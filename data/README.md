# Real study data used by TraceIT v5

The initial record is **not synthetic**.

`mansagar_2019_2020.csv` reproduces the four-site monthly measurements reported by Sharma & Choudhary, *Assessment of Water Quality Parameters of Man Sagar Lake Jaipur, Rajasthan, India* (2021): 4 sites × 4 months, November 2019–February 2020. The paper reports the sampling coordinates, temperature, pH, TDS, dissolved oxygen, hardness, turbidity, acidity, alkalinity, chloride, fluoride, nitrate, BOD and COD.

`mansagar_2021_2023_summary.csv` contains the summary values reported by Anamika, Kumari & Meena (2025) for a separate two-year study of four sites and 576 samples. It is kept as a contextual research layer, not merged into the older time series.

Sources:

- https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352402146_Assessment_of_Water_Quality_Parameters_of_Man_Sagar_Lake_Jaipur_Rajasthan_India
- https://ijzab.com/announce/download/653
- https://data.gov.in/catalog/water-quality-india-2013

Important: these are historical research measurements. TraceIT does **not** label them as current 2026 water quality. Current live context is obtained separately from GBIF and Open-Meteo when refreshed.
